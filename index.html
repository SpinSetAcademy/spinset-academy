About SpinSet Academy

SpinSet Academy was created to help young tennis players develop confidence, movement, consistency, and strong fundamentals in a positive and motivating environment.


Coach Sanav has been playing tennis since age 4 and focuses on helping beginner and intermediate players improve through structured training, discipline, movement development, and match-focused coaching.


SpinSet Academy combines high-energy training with fun learning experiences so players continue improving while enjoying the game and building long-term confidence on the court.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you coach?

SpinSet Academy primarily coaches beginner and intermediate youth players.

Do students need previous tennis experience?

No. Beginner lessons are designed for players who are completely new to tennis.

What should students bring?

Students should bring a tennis racket, athletic shoes, and a water bottle.

Where are lessons held?

Lessons are currently held at Old Settlers Park Tennis Courts in Round Rock, Texas.
