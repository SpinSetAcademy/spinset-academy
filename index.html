About SpinSet Academy

SpinSet Academy was created to help young players develop confidence, movement, consistency, and strong tennis fundamentals in a positive and motivating training environment.


Coach Sanav has been playing tennis since age 4 and focuses on helping beginner and intermediate players improve through structured lessons, discipline, movement training, and match-focused development.


SpinSet Academy is designed to make tennis training both fun and challenging so students continue improving while enjoying the game and building long-term confidence on the court.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you coach?

SpinSet Academy primarily coaches beginner and intermediate youth players.

Do students need previous tennis experience?

No. Beginner lessons are designed for players who are new to tennis.

What should students bring?

Students should bring a tennis racket, athletic shoes, and a water bottle.

Where are lessons held?

Lessons are currently held at Old Settlers Park Tennis Courts in Round Rock, Texas.
