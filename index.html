Why SpinSet Exists

My name is Sanav, and I have been playing tennis since I was 4 years old. Over the years, tennis has shaped my discipline, mindset, and confidence. I created SpinSet Academy to share that experience with other players who want to improve, compete, and grow into stronger athletes.

This academy is built on real passion for the game — not just training, but building players who think, move, and compete like athletes.
